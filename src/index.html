Sources and data notes

—

Sana: Trybe bookings and sales. Restaurant: Silverware POS. Staffing: Push clock reports (hours only; no names held here). Profit: general ledger April–September 2026. Each figure is copied from the source dashboards, which this page follows when they are refreshed.

Trybe counts gift cards, memberships and pass bundles as sales when sold, not when used. POS sales exclude tax and tips.
